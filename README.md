# Sreenivasatrailors

Act as an expert UI/UX designer and frontend developer specializing in modernizing outdated legacy websites.

Your goal is to perform a complete visual and structural redesign of the business detailed below, using their existing website as a baseline for brand content and media, but applying a ultra-modern, high-converting design system.

Existing Site & Asset Handling:
Scrape & Reference Existing Content: Access the provided existing website URL ([EXISTING_WEBSITE_URL]). Analyze its branding, services, and photo assets.

Media Extraction: Re-use high-quality original images, logos, and specific photos found on the old website. If an image is low resolution, upscale/enhance its layout framing (e.g., placing it inside sleek modern glassmorphism cards, device mockups, or rounded image grids with subtle overlays).

Design & Aesthetic Guidelines:
Visual Style: Modern, sleek, clean, and premium feel (Neumorphism/Glassmorphism accents, subtle background gradients, generous padding, and crisp typography). Completely overhaul outdated tables, old fonts, or crowded layouts.

Animations & Interactivity:

Smooth entry animations (fade-in, slide-up) as the user scrolls.

Interactive hover effects on cards, buttons, and call-to-actions (CTAs).

Micro-interactions and smooth transitions using Lucide icons and Framer Motion.

Color Palette: Extract and elevate the core brand colors from their existing logo/site, pairing them with dark or light sleek neutral backgrounds for a modern look.

Core Structure & Business Essentials:
Hero Section:

Bold modern headline, sub-headline, primary CTA ("Book Now" / "Get Started"), and secondary CTA ("Explore Our Services").

Highlight trust indicators (e.g., Google star rating, badge for "Serving the area since X").

About & Heritage:

Modern narrative section highlighting the business history pulled from the old site, framed nicely with photos.
Services / Features Showcase:

Clean grid or tabbed card layout showing core offerings with subtle hover zoom effects.
Social Proof & Reviews:

Google Maps review score badges and styled testimonial cards pulling actual customer quotes.
Interactive Location & Operating Hours:

Dynamic operating hours card with an active status badge ("Open Now" / "Closed").

Address details with a direct "Get Directions" button.

Footer & Quick Contact:

Tap-to-call phone number, email, address, and social links.:
